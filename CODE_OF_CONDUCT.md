# OsaintMaint Code of Conduct

## 🤝 Our promise

We want OsaintMaint to be a **respectful, practical, and privacy-minded**
open-source project. Everyone who participates — whether contributing code,
opening issues, or giving feedback — should be able to do so without harassment
or intimidation.

---

## ✅ Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient
- Discuss ideas, evidence, and implementation — not people
- **Protect privacy**: do not post personal data, credentials, private-source
  material, or sensitive investigation details anywhere in the project
- Keep contributions compatible with lawful, authorized public-data research
- Help make the project clearer, safer, and more useful for the next contributor

---

## ❌ Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or personal attacks
- Doxxing, sharing private information, or pressuring someone to expose sensitive details
- Promoting credential abuse, access-control bypasses, private-system access, or person targeting
- Disruptive behavior that prevents others from participating in good faith
- Publishing sensitive security or vulnerability details in public issues

---

## 📣 Reporting a concern

For **routine community concerns**, contact a project maintainer through the
repository using a minimal, privacy-safe message.

For a **security vulnerability**, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
rather than publishing details in a public issue or discussion.

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other appropriate
action to protect the community and the project.
